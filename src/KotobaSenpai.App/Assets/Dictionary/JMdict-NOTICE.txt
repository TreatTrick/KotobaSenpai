JMdict English dictionary data

Copyright James William Breen and the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG).

KotobaSenpai uses the JMdict English data for offline word lookup and reading
selection. JmdictIndexBuilder converts the English JSON from jmdict-simplified
into a SQLite index of written forms, readings, and senses. The resulting
jmdict.db is distributed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International (CC BY-SA 4.0).
