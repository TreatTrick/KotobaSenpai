# Website design

Visitors are browsing a bright product page before returning to a visual novel; a white canvas keeps the dark game demonstrations legible and gives the playful companion room to stand out.

The reference is Apple's product storytelling: large centered headings, generous spacing, sticky navigation, immersive product media, and purposeful scroll transitions. Retain KotobaSenpai's existing dragon-maid icon and violet hair color as the identity. Use white and lightly tinted neutral surfaces, charcoal text, and a restrained violet accent. Use OKLCH color tokens and a self-hosted Manrope variable font with native CJK fallbacks.

The narrative moves through the original-language story, annotated reading, contextual understanding, language choice, provider setup and cost, onboarding, and download. Use real media in several different compositions rather than repeating feature cards.

Motion includes a choreographed hero, subtle screen perspective, scroll emphasis, a language gallery, and the original Clutch mascot interaction. Default HTML content remains visible; animations are progressive enhancements. Reduced motion disables scroll transforms and autoplay. Offscreen and hidden-tab video playback pauses.

Keep content panels at 12–16 px corners. Navigation blur is used only to keep the sticky header readable over moving product imagery. Body text must remain comfortably legible at mobile widths and meet AA contrast.
