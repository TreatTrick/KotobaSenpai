# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

Introduce KotobaSenpai to people reading Japanese visual novels. Help visitors understand the in-game reading experience, see real demonstrations, and download the Windows application.

## Positioning

Stay with the story while Japanese readings, pitch accents, contextual explanations, and translations stay beside the original text.

## Conversion and proof

The primary action downloads the published v1.0.0 installer. Secondary actions open the source repository and release notes. Proof comes from the existing GIF demonstrations and screenshots in `../docs/assets`, not invented testimonials or benchmark claims. The documented cost example is one 30-minute session of Witch on the Holy Night with DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, not a guaranteed rate.

## Brand personality

Calm, clear, playful. The user requested Apple-like simplicity and animation, alongside the exact interactive dragon maid from `D:/project/Clutch/website`.

## Design principles

- Demonstrate the real application before explaining details.
- Give one idea room to breathe in each chapter.
- Make the download easy to find throughout the page.
- Use animation to explain reading and interaction.
- Preserve keyboard access and reduced-motion alternatives.

## Accessibility and localization

Prerender English and Simplified Chinese pages. Keep download and language links usable without JavaScript. Provide video pause controls, visible focus, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion handling. Do not add analytics or collect credentials.
